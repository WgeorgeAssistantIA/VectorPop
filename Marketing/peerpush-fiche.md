## Description produit (EN)
VectorPop converts PNG, JPEG, BMP and WEBP images into clean, editable SVG, vector PDF, or high-resolution PNG. Live preview while you tune the result, background removal (flat-color or AI-powered), one-click auto-tune that tests multiple settings and picks the best match to your source image, and batch processing for entire folders.

100% local: your images never leave your computer, no cloud upload. Built for designers, print-on-demand sellers, and anyone who needs to turn a raster logo or artwork into a real scalable vector without redrawing it by hand in Illustrator or Inkscape.

Free tier: unlimited vectorization, all presets, flat-color background removal, 3 SVG exports/day. Pro: unlimited exports, PDF/high-res PNG, AI background removal, auto-tune, batch processing — one-time €39, no subscription (Windows).
