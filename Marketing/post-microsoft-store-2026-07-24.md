## LinkedIn (EN)

🎉 VectorPop is now available on the Microsoft Store!

A few weeks ago I launched VectorPop: a desktop app that turns a pixelated logo (PNG/JPEG) into a clean, editable vector file (SVG) — no Illustrator or Inkscape required.

The need behind it was simple: I was tired of manually re-tracing client logos when all I had was a low-res image.

What's new with this Store release:
✅ One-click install, automatic updates
✅ Same engine (vtracer) + gradient reconstruction + background removal
✅ Still 100% local — no image ever leaves your computer

This is the 3rd app from my studio (alongside VoxCut and InOneShot) to land on the Microsoft Store this year. The ecosystem keeps growing, one app at a time 🚀
